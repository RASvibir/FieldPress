1. What this product is

FieldPress is a pocket newsroom for indie journalists. Capture in the field, then produce three outputs with Gemini: a publication-ready article, a social thread or caption, and a podcast script with show notes.

Produce is not a recap bot. It pulls live public headlines (related to the story, national conversation, global/international, plus what the public is looking up) so the drafts can land with people who are already paying attention. Field notes remain the only source of original facts.

  • Public surface: Open / Install / Copy link / QR, plus the public user guide at /guide.
  • Newsroom: /app — stories, produce, edit, distribute. Public GUIDE only — never this operator book.
  • This operator manual (unlisted): /admin, /admin-manual.html, /admin-manual.md. Not linked from launch or the newsroom. Bookmark it. Search engines are told to skip it.

3. First-run setup

  1. Node 24+ and pnpm. From the repo root: pnpm install.
  2. Copy .env.example to .env. Fill Neon URLs and GEMINI_API_KEY. Never commit .env.
  3. pnpm db:push if tables are missing (stories, story_items, drafts).
  4. pnpm dev for API + Vite, or pnpm start for a single port.
  5. Confirm GET /api/healthz and GET /api/dashboard.
  6. Install the PWA from the launch page so the phone opens the newsroom without browser chrome.
  7. On Vercel, set the same env names on the fieldpress project, then redeploy. Production alias: https://fieldpress-dusky.vercel.app.
Deploy from this machine: vercel deploy --prod --yes --scope tuffputer. A deploy without the team scope can fail with “Not authorized.”

4. Daily desk

  1. Open. Production launch page → OPEN APP, or the installed icon. Installed (standalone) skips launch and goes to /app.
  2. New story. Headline is the search seed for public trends. Prefer “Emu on Main, Danville IL” over “weird animal.” 255 character max.
  3. Notes. Add what you saw, names you can stand behind, quotes, times, places, what you could not confirm. Thin notes produce thin drafts.
  4. Import (optional). Paste a mobile dispatch. First line becomes the title (strips # or DISPATCH:). Later lines become notes.
  5. AI PRODUCE. 10–40 seconds. Scans live headlines, then writes article, social, and podcast. Existing drafts for those three modes are overwritten.
  6. Trend Desk. Read Why now, Audience, and the relatable / national / global hooks before you touch the copy. These are assignment notes, not witnessed facts. Stored in this browser only.
  7. Edit. Open each draft. Autosave. Click a source note or a trend hook in the sidebar to insert it. Cut anything the notes do not support.
  8. Distribute. One draft or the whole package: copy, save markdown, native share, or compose on X, Threads, Bluesky, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, email.
AI Produce replaces the latest article, social, and podcast drafts for that story. Copy out anything you still need before you hit it again.

5. How to write notes that produce

The model is only as good as the notes. Put the exclusive on the page.

  • One scene a reader can see (sound, weather, a person’s hands, a closed street).
  • Attributable quotes in quotation marks. Unnamed sources labeled as such.
  • Numbers you counted or an official gave you — and who gave them.
  • Time and place. “Tonight, 4th and Main” beats “earlier.”
  • What you do not know. Produce will say what still needs verification if you write it down.
  • Do not dump a national recap into notes and expect an exclusive. The exclusive is what you witnessed.

6. Trend desk and AI Produce

Produce does two jobs: look at the public conversation, then write drafts that use that conversation for framing only.

LayerWhat it isWhere it lives
Field notesOnly source of original facts, quotes, names, counts, on-scene detail.Postgres story_items
Live headlinesGoogle News related search (last 7 days), US national, world, Wikipedia most-read.Fetched at produce time; fail-soft
Optional Gemini searchGEMINI_TREND_SEARCH=1 adds a live web assignment brief. Uses extra quota. Off by default.Gemini, then discarded
Trend DeskWhy now, audience, 3–6 hooks with a productHook for article/social/podcast.This browser, localStorage key fieldpress.trendDesk.<storyId>
DraftsArticle, social, podcast. Overwritten on each successful produce.Postgres drafts

How the three scales should be used

  • Relatable: everyday stakes, the question a non-expert would ask, one human scene.
  • National: institutions, policy, who holds power, what country-level coverage is missing.
  • Global: a parallel or knock-on that keeps an overseas listener in the episode.

Drafts should: article nut graf places the reporting in public conversation; social opens on a hook people already recognize, then the exclusive field detail; podcast cold open poses the national/global question, then drops into the scene. Show notes must label public context as public context.

Never let a draft present a headline as something the reporter observed. If a trend is only loosely related, drop it. Verify every public-context claim before publish.

Switching phones or browsers: drafts are in the database; Trend Desk is not. Produce again on the new device, or work from the drafts.

Retries: Gemini 429 and 503 are retried twice with backoff. Headline fetch failures do not abort produce.

7. Edit, templates, and distribute

Editor

  • Autosave 1.5 seconds after the last keystroke. Status line shows SAVED / UNSAVED.
  • Click a source item to append it to the draft. Click a Trend Desk hook to append the product hook.
  • Article template: HEADLINE / BYLINE / DATELINE → lede → body → nut graf → kicker.
  • Social: thread posts separated by a line with only ---, or a single caption.
  • Podcast: cold open, intro, segments with timestamps, outro, show notes.

8. Phone / Expo field app

pnpm dev:mobile

Scan the QR with Expo Go. On a real device, EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL must be the computer’s LAN IP (example http://192.168.1.10:3000), not localhost. Phone and computer on the same network.

  • Story list, archive/restore, text notes, audio recording.
  • AI Producer screen calls POST /api/produce and shows summary, Trend Desk, outline, caption.
  • Export dispatch via the system share sheet, then paste into desktop IMPORT.

Native store builds: from artifacts/fieldpress, npx eas-cli login, npx eas-cli init, then npx eas-cli build --platform ios or android.

10. Data model

  • stories — id, title (255), status active|archived, timestamps.
  • story_items — type note|audio|photo, content (note body, audio URI, or caption), belongs to story, cascade delete.
  • drafts — mode article|social|podcast, title (500), content, belongs to story, cascade delete. Produce upserts the latest row per mode.

There is no login. Anyone with the URL can read and write the newsroom. Treat the production URL as a desk tool, not a public CMS, until you add auth.

pnpm start        # build desktop + API, serve :3000

docker build -t fieldpress .
docker run --rm -p 3000:3000 --env-file .env fieldpress
vercel deploy --prod --yes --scope tuffputer

Health after ship: curl -sS https://fieldpress-dusky.vercel.app/api/healthz. Manual file: https://fieldpress-dusky.vercel.app/admin-manual.html must be the paper page, not the React shell.

If produce is slow, that is normal. If it fails with 429, the Gemini project is out of quota — drafts will not generate until billing/rate limits recover. Leave trend search off.

14. Before you publish

  1. Every name, title, and count is in the notes or a named public source you checked.
  2. Quotes are accurate. Unnamed material is labeled.
  3. Why-now and trend hooks are framed as public context, not as reporting from the scene.
  4. Social hashtags match the real conversation. Kill generic #News if that is all you have.
  5. Podcast show notes distinguish “this week’s conversation” from your tape.
  6. You would read this aloud to the people in the notes without flinching.

15. Do not

  • Do not publish unverified names, casualty counts, or quotes.
  • Do not treat Trend Desk headlines as reporting from the scene.
  • Do not put this manual, keys, or operator URLs on the launch page, the public user guide, or README.
  • Do not commit .env, .vercel, or API keys.
  • Do not brand FieldPress as another studio’s product. This newsroom stands alone.
  • Do not assume the newsroom is private just because the manual is unlisted. There is no login yet.

Cheat sheet

Specific headline → notes with a scene and attributable quotes → AI PRODUCE → read Why now / audience / three-scale hooks → edit article, social, podcast → verify → Distribute → keep a markdown copy → only then delete or overwrite.
